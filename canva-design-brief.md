# Canva Design Brief — CodeNest Academy

Visual direction for turning the captions into Canva posts.

## Brand look
| Element | Choice |
|---|---|
| Primary colour | Navy `#1B2A6B` |
| Accent colour | Orange `#E8711A` |
| Background | Off-white `#F2F5FC` |
| Headline font | Montserrat Bold (or Poppins Bold) |
| Body font | Inter / Open Sans |
| Style | Clean, code-editor inspired, lots of white space |

## Post sizes in Canva
| Platform | Canva preset | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram post / carousel | Instagram Post (Portrait) | 1080 × 1350 |
| Reels / Shorts / Story | Instagram Story | 1080 × 1920 |
| LinkedIn | LinkedIn Post | 1200 × 1200 |
| X (Twitter) | Twitter Post | 1600 × 900 |

## Rules
- Keep the CTA visible on the image as well as in the caption.
- Max ~8 words of text on any single graphic.
- Logo bottom-right, small, on every post.
